# Health Facility Access in Wamako LGA, Sokoto State

A GIS project brief.

## 1. The question

Which wards in Wamako LGA, Sokoto State are 5 km from a health facility?

## 2. Why it matters

The question identifies wards in Wamako LGA where residents must travel more than 5 km to reach the nearest health facility, which highlights gaps in physical access.

Local Government health officials and primary healthcare coordinators can use these findings to prioritise the siting of new facilities and to focus road improvement on underserved wards.

## 3. The data needed

1. Nigeria ward-level data
2. Nigeria LGA-level data
3. Nigeria state-level data
4. Health facilities in Wamako LGA, Sokoto State, Nigeria
5. Road data for Wamako LGA, Sokoto State, Nigeria

## 5. What I would build

An interactive web map of Wamako LGA that shows every ward coloured by its distance to the nearest health facility, with wards beyond 5 km clearly flagged in red.

The map can be updated each month as new facility or road data is added. Local health officials can open it on any phone or computer and immediately see which communities need priority.
